## Takeaways (Granola)

From Granola Editing Notes "Editing Ellis Hamburger" (2025-03-18) Takeaways (primary gold):

- In tech there’s a big opportunity to lead with story > product
- “words and ideas are design”
- Writing at The Verge taught him how to turn a company into a single sentence headline
- At Snapchat he owned blog posts, release notes, app store description, Spectacles packaging, every product string, first TV commercial, S-1 before IPO
- Brand was invented to differentiate commodity products → tech is saturated now so brand matters more than ever (even more true with AI)
- Josh Miller (Browser Co) differentiated a commodity browser via philosophies, ideas, max transparency; Snap did brand campaigns every ~1.5 years
- “Helping founders find their truth” / find meaning in a long list of features
- Stories align product strategy not just marketing (Raycast Focus Mode once messaging locked on flow)
- “Ideas > Features”
- Copy: communicate you understand the problem first, then present the solution; headline asks the question, subhead answers clearly
- Keep asking “why does that matter?” to push past faster/better
- “we are the best way to _____ so that you can _____” (second blank is the important one)
- Ideas create multiple touch points more easily than features
