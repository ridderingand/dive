## Takeaways (Granola)

Granola-weak: Notion episode Scratchpad while editing (no Granola Editing note). Normalized from that scratchpad:

### Respect the process
- Not one-shot “generate a website” — sitemap → wireframe → Figma → Webflow with designer in the driver’s seat
- AI as assistant; milestones/checkpoints matter for client work

### Speed of iteration / taste
- Generate multiple options (e.g. 3 about pages); critique and select
- Point AI at page, section, component, or line of copy
- Role shifts toward critiquing AI output and combining concepts

### Research + create
- AI brings industry context; research and design can collapse into one step (still need research, but learning while creating)

### Component-driven
- Built on ~1000+ components mapped to Figma/Webflow best practices + variables/styles
- Production gap small vs blank-slate generators; responsive out of the box
