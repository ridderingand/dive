## Takeaways (Granola)

From Granola prep notes "Ridd <> Chris Abad" (2025-01-27) — **no dedicated "Editing Chris Abad" note**; using prep notes as taste layer:

- Operator for ~10 years; storytelling strength consistently in feedback
- Presentations often backwards: people use slides as cue cards; start with what you’ll say, not visuals
- Context first: audience + desired outcome + main point (“What is the one thing you want out of this?”)
- Dry run + record transcript; write the words / storyboard before visuals
- Mentors early-career designers on portfolio storytelling; reframe work as more senior/strategic
- People walk away with a sentence of takeaways — simplify complex into concise message
- Storytelling in high-stakes presentations vs micro (pitching a sketch to a PM next day)
- Turning point: Story 10x book — specific arc for product stories
- Prefer hiring simplifiers over complexifiers
