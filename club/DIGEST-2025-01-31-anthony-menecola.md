## Takeaways (Granola)

- Being a staff designer is making impact outside your assigned area (“broadening your T”) — look for tangential opportunities; get to higher altitude / bigger picture; Anthony broadened his T by getting more technical
- Staff archetypes: historian (accumulated context; first-party apps initiative); design systems (component/system wizard); organizational thinker (which parts of the business should work together / platform strategy — big swings that take time)
- Becoming more technical: no dedicated QA; see the “shape of code”; fixing a corner radius / type styles is empowering; **Cursor moved him past polish PRs into explorations and prototypes at the code level**
- Relationships: get into PMs’ brains on long-term strategy; scratch file of topics that keep coming up = backlog for future-facing explorations; designer helps product realize a vision
- Async: write a short script before Looms so you stay concise instead of a 10-minute ramble
