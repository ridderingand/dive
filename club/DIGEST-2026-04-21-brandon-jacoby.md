## Summary

Brandon Jacoby (ex–Cash App growth design, Party Round / Capital, X/Twitter; now independent) walks Ridd through Elon-era decisiveness at X, Nikita’s onboarding crash course, going solo as AI tools made autonomy newly viable, who actually gets amplified by AI, and the hard jump from *seeing* taste to *creating* it. Core claims: questioning every requirement is a lived practice you can’t textbook; AI amplifies designers who already run through walls, not people who externalize blockers; seeing taste is widespread — creating taste is knowing when to break patterns vs. follow them; software is hospitality (welcoming users into a product) and in a world of easy good-enough the scarce offer is how to be great; craft and consequence are not mutually exclusive; 0→1 magic starts with ugly first versions and tinkering between prompts. Why it matters: a clear taste/craft frame for solo design practice as models flatten “can build” — and a practical spectrum of who AI actually lifts.

Descript speakers are already labeled **Brandon** (guest) and **Ridd** (host); no Speaker-N remapping. Timecodes and section markers are trusted from Descript (no ±30s caveat).

## Takeaways (Granola)

- Decisiveness of Elon at X (question everything)

- Difference between seeing taste and creating taste
