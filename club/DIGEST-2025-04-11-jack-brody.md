## Takeaways (Granola)

From Granola Editing Notes "Editing Jack Brody" (2025-04-10) Takeaways (primary gold):

- Early Snap prioritized emergent behavior over feedback — what were people "hacking" that wasn't native? (geofilter + "come hang" → Snap Map).
- Snap pioneered swipe-based navigation; AI is another chance to question everything — do things differently to do them better.
- You have to fail a lot to find big 10x winners — Evan created an environment where you could be wrong a lot; be right in extremely counterintuitive ways.
- Telling stories is how you drive impact — chart the future, evidence, compelling narrative across disciplines.
- Kim Kardashian visited the office (Face Swap inventor bit — fun short; ideas are cheap, execution matters).
- Startups birthed from AI get to build around it from first principles — legacy cos bandaid AI into current ways of working.
- Building tech in search of a problem — capabilities emerge you didn't anticipate; AI softens the old rule.
- Designers can look for opportunities to collect preference data — every two songs is a preference signal.
- AI as creativity launchpad — don't describe what you want it to look like first; difference is a feature.
- When to not listen to feedback — feedback is a gift, but designers needn't incorporate every (or any) "this is dumb" note if conviction is deep.
