## Takeaways (Granola)

- Senior ≠ volume of execution ownership; proposing ideas / creating momentum = senior; early energy matters
- Rubik's cube analogy (possible hook)
- Staff = take an idea, grow it, navigate the org autonomously so it can make business impact
- Create appetite, don't sell solutions
- Systems thinking: show what a feature enables; more surface area → more people excited; identify building blocks
- Make the jump by picking a single idea and going for it (or ask senior wishlist for a design sprint)
- Visualize founder vision (Patreon / Jack Conte)
