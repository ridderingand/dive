## Takeaways (Granola)

### Designing for LLMs
- Small pointed apps > open-ended chatbots that dump responsibility on users
- Goal-oriented flows; AI as advanced search in purpose-built software
- Guide through vs magical answer; first-principles interfaces
- Cognitive/sense-making AI more interesting than generative
- Compositional small tasks → complex outputs

### Coding / artifacts
- Sending preview links > Figma links (need live results; hard to fake with LLMs)
- Understand "shape of code"; web as dynamic system with inputs
- Design engineering = holistic software design, not mockups-only
