## Takeaways (Granola)

No Granola Editing note — normalized from Notion Written Takeaways:

- Prepare before a layoff: walking deck of recent work separate from website; don’t block announcement on portfolio scramble
- Announcement post with intentional asks (tag hiring, recommendations, coffee chats) — “closed mouths don’t get fed”
- Cast a wide net first for interview data, then go after top-list companies; get reps before crucial conversations
- Job hunt coach: notes after every conversation; ask “how did this go / anything missing?”; iterate deck; fill gaps async
- Interview after the offer to reverse the pressure; negotiate with Levels.fyi coach
- Lean into personal story (“human beyond the pixels”) so you are memorable for you
- Marathon not sprint; find balance early — “no” wasn’t in vocabulary; burnout has compounding effects
- Save everything including graveyard/unshipped work — often more impressive than shipped metrics pieces
