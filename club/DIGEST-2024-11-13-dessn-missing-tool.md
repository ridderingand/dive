## Takeaways (Granola)

- AI product strategy: QA as an entry point for teams with engineers; the question is whether designers can push code to production, not whether they can code
- Build around a vision and core principles rather than a rigid six-month roadmap; model changes can alter the landscape overnight
- Choosing a wedge means avoiding “short hills,” including saying no to early revenue when it pulls the product toward work that may not persist
- AI could make design systems less centralized, support self-optimizing UI, and move designers toward curation and creative direction
