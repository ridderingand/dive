## Takeaways (Granola)

Granola-weak: Notion episode Content ideas while editing (no dedicated Granola Editing note / Scratchpad). Normalized from that note — do not invent a Granola note:

### Business acuity
- Knock on doors of business meetings; first year: listen, notebook of WAU/NSM/ARR unknowns
- Anticipate "What do you think we should do?" — form a POV on roadmap
- Ask better data questions; treat data as playful hunting for stories

### Onboarding
- Don't make big bets with multiple variables; minimize investments and isolate variables
- Onboarding isn't just signup/tooltips — can last weeks; research: 60%+ products rely on tooltips
- Email is a huge part of onboarding experience design
- Identify aha / activation metric

### Emotion vs problems
- Design for shifting user emotions (worry/anxiety → another emotion), not only problem-solving
