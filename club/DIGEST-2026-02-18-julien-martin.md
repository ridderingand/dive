## Summary

Julien Martin (design co-founder of Zenly and Amo; now in talent matching via Off Market) traces a craft-first arc from Behance apprenticeship to consumer-app design DNA, then to how lean founding-designer hiring works today. Arc: Behance lore (Strasbourg → illegal NYC tourist → Mathias Corea / Scott Belsky apartment office, unpaid 7-day hustle, pixel icons before vectors) → Matias’s Bauhaus-strict craft and “feel it then narrow” speed → Zenly as playful map social with game DNA (colorful ice-cream logo, blue-everything sandbox, overdesigned mood messages, hollow effect from logo/icons into UI) → Snap shut Zenly down; Amo as reaction — suite of three apps to escape Christmas-tree feature creep → early luxury/onyx direction killed after ~4 months as cold → atom found in animated real-you avatars → fridge-magnet collage brand → delight as interaction strategy (Rolodex/iPod friend wheel, not confetti) → novelty vs usability balance and word-of-mouth → culture of side-by-side eng+design jamming and gut feel without years of data → hiring signal = side projects over FAANG résumé → Off Market job-market read: teams staying at ~7 for years, founding designer as 360/AI-fluent/code-in-repo role, big-tech alumni discounted unless they prove startup hunger → taste shoutouts (Retro, Duolingo).
