Noam Segal (leads the AI Insights team at Figma; prior Dive Club episode 2024-05-09, `TYd5W8_SgAk`; article https://noamsegal.substack.com/p/research-slop-is-human-slop, linked in the YouTube description and alluded to when he says "mentioned research slop earlier" at 00:44:40, though the title is not spoken in this cut) walks Ridd through how research is changing now that the old constraints are gone: who you talk to (including digital twins), who does the research (self-serve, with guardrails), when and where it happens (ambient / rapid / possibly auto-spun studies), and how insights actually reach the people who need them.
