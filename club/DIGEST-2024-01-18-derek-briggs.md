### Craft culture traits
- Leadership must care about output and finer details (Derek designs + pushes code while leading)
- Dedicated UI engineering team as hiring requirement to attract design talent
- Fluid handoff: engineers often make design *better* instead of cutting corners
- Senior people / flatter stacks (less handholding, less process)
- Magnet for talent: FE specialists want hard frontend problems; designers want engineers who care
- Smaller teams (PlanetScale: one designer + one developer)
- Limited feedback culture — most feedback between designer and developer; high trust

### Craft flywheel (business case)
- More time per feature / specialist salaries → attract best talent → more autonomy → less process → higher velocity → lower total cost per feature
- Pride when the work is truly *yours*
