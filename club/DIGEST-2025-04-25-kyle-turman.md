## Takeaways (Granola)

From Granola Editing Notes "Editing Kyle Turman" (2025-04-24) Takeaways (primary gold):

- First full-time product designer at Anthropic.
- Startup designers have to exist at multiple elevations simultaneously — front-end code, UI, marketing, social assets, strategy, naming.
- Designers don't talk about emotion enough — every interaction has feeling; you can't design something people love without a few people hating it.
- Art vs design: it is and isn't — a bit of you fossilizes into early product DNA (connects to Tuhin's ideas on art).
- Figma becomes less valuable in a non-deterministic world — early designs half-finished, just enough to get into the codebase.
- It's egotistical to say we need a new UX paradigm than chat — explored hundreds of patterns; chat wins on accessibility + iteration; designer explores then mirrors the org.
- When design is prompting — model-first thinking; sometimes need prompt improvements more than design features (hospital / SaaS REST analogy).
- How to build context within an LLM — "Where are we at?" teammate vs stranger; models need the human's current experience context.
- Company rituals: personal Slack notebook channels for WIP, ideas, and discussion (Artifacts origin).
