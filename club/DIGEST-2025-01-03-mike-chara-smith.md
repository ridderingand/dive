## Takeaways (Granola)

Source: Granola "Editing Smith & Diction" (2024-12-30) id=0c2ae09b-28e2-4c8d-abbe-8c63987b18a8

Thoughtful identities for imaginative clients

STAR What person embodies this brand?
- Perplexity should feel like Malcolm Gladwell
- Sometimes they send the kids to grandma's house, open a bottle of wine, and just start throwing out people that might unlock a deeper level of the brand identity
  - this gives you personality and tone
  - Simone Biles might be determined, focused, measured, accurate, etc.
    - these types of words can help you go into a different territory (ex: "accurate" wouldn't come to mind)
  - "it's very easy to use words like tidy and clean, but are those words you'd use to describe your best friend?"

STAR Too often corporate strategy has shareholders in mind. That's why when they're working on a vision for a company they imagine a prospective employee reading it while considering accepting a job offer. How can they make that person feel something? What would it take for them to cancel their other interviews and accept the job on the spot?
- "It shouldn't just be a plan… it should be a thing that makes somebody feel something"

STAR Need moves
- Things that you can do with the brand that feel ownable
- Bathroom test: type out "Bathroom is closed" and with nothing but the type on the page does it still feel on brand?
- Agency work for universities like Duke and Notre Dame: logo/colors steeped in history so signature elements exist outside logo/color.

THINK ABOUT BRAND AS INGREDIENTS
- A brand isn't a thing you make and you're done with it. A brand is like food… you have to make it again every single day. The moves you have are like ingredients in your kitchen.
- Sometimes you make a quesadilla. It's simple—just tortilla and cheese—but it's SO good.

STAR Copywriting hack
If you're writing copy for a website and you're stuck try writing something completely different like a poem or a story about the brand
- Star Wars intro crawl with 0 intention of using it — force ideas.
- Each medium you write for produces a new style.
