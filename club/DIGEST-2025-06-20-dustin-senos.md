## Summary

Dustin Senos (The Browser Company; Dia / Arc) on designing an AI-native browser after Arc’s novelty-heavy north star. Arc came from “what if browsers were different”; Dia spends the novelty budget on obvious-feeling innovation — chat placement, spatial metaphors, skills as building blocks for future agents, personalization at the context/memory layer (opt-in).
