## Takeaways (Granola)

Granola-weak: Notion episode Scratchpad while editing (no Granola Editing note). Normalized from that scratchpad — do not invent a Granola note:

### Half-day fidelity
- "Whatever you can get done in half a day → that is the fidelity"
- Gray box or high-fi doesn't matter; don't spend more than half a day laying the foundation for a new idea

### Founder taste vs familiarity
- Early social features were "brute force" and got scrapped; now smaller unique flair with less pressure to always innovate
- Spotify / health data shipped because Dennis thinks it's "super cool" / "fun things" — rarely added post-revenue, so snuck in early

### Singular vision / website
- Pictured entire website on a run; didn't ask for feedback
- Believer in singular vision; more cooks → harder consistency
- Website as statement of who they are; zig when others zag

### How to add soul
- Sweat the details beyond the brief; obsessive iteration + feedback to engineers
- Add personality (copy, fun features); hard to bolt on after PMF
- Draw inspiration outside web/UI patterns (games, fashion)

### Feedback
- One precise user description of something broken is enough to course-correct (~18 min) — most users say nothing
