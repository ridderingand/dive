## Summary

Ryan Scott (DoorDash / Airbnb alum; Maven instructor) reframes promotion as a business-impact skill, not a craft ceiling. Arc: today’s hiring market over-filters for narrow puzzle-piece profiles → designers sell craft to design managers while product leaders hold final yes → sell impact in business language (metrics + competitive position + efficiency/velocity/culture/ownership) → AI collapses roles so designers must move toward PM *and* eng territory, with the strategic/product path under-taught vs vibe-coding → find opportunities (DoorDash early: live order tracking from Amplitude outliers; Dasher Instant Pay from empathy) → buy-in via low-fi evidence with smaller groups without skipping levels → prioritization as value/cost/risk not only RICE → make data a practice by asking PMs what the team is trying to achieve. Core claims: craft gets you hired, business impact gets you promoted; impact beyond conversion; behavioral outliers beat dashboards-as-math; don’t stand still. Why it matters: clearest Fall/Winter 2025 career-strategy playbook for designers squeezed by AI-era role collapse.
