## Takeaways (Granola)

From Granola Editing Notes "Editing Granola Sam Stephenson" (2025-03-26) Takeaways (primary gold):

- Chris realized ChatGPT/GPT-3 would change everything in technology but it wasn't obvious how to apply it.
- Early research: interview ~2 dozen people — "fishing for problems in people's days"; calendar hour-by-hour walkthroughs for straight facts; observing what people actually do ≠ asking what they think they do.
- Key trait of startup designers: not being attached to your ideas — tab-autocomplete real-time partner felt more sci-fi than Granola today but failed presence.
- Ruthless attention to the core flow — edge features (templates, transcript) can stay undiscoverable for months.
- New products have to have an opinion — deliberately bury the transcript in a tiny box.
- Separate making a useful product from making one that's easy to understand (David Lee) — hand-onboard every user on video; ~150 beta users before onboarding work; retention is the only early signal.
- Looking for a designer to take prototyping, exploration, and a bunch of wrong turns — early-stage designers wear both hats.
- Know when you're in explore mode vs execute with tried-and-true patterns (e.g. cmd+K search).
- Big design problems aren't pixel problems — fork the app and live in real code (~47 min).
