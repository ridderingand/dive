## Summary

Ron Goldin (design leader; Uber Eats courier experience, Shopify online checkout, now building side products including Matchmaker/MTCHMKR with his wife) walks Ridd through how research empathy and zero-to-one building show up as a design leader — then how AI changed the manager job, the job market, and the org shape. Arc: first-week dogfooding on Uber Eats (city bike deliveries → international courier rides with Insta360 + Google Cardboard) → cut the “sitting in a car waiting” feeling into an all-hands video that sparked a company dogfooding program and hotspot tooling → Shopify checkout at inflection (Toby wants it better; OpenAI partnership, Coinbase crypto, early Figma MCP ↔ Cursor) → hackathon NFC sticker → POS buy-and-walk-out prototype → manager role flattening into player-coach where prototypes win arguments in minutes between meetings → vibe-coding a brand-color/logo SVG tool after a VP said merchants wouldn’t / couldn’t do it → job-market signal that every director seat wants hands-on (one startup watched him use Auto Layout with an Eastern Europe engineer) and treats personal builds like Matchmaker as hiring evidence → Matchmaker: Softr MVP → Lovable so non-technical cofounders can run it; “build wireframe” then Figma sticker sheet ingested for high-fi → process now: ugly PRD → Claude prompt plan → phased sprints → project memory → dual-task Figma when words fail → brand/voice early so products don’t look identical → dream org: product thinking + scrappy research + brand voice + enough CS to debug agent loops; brand engineers / motion as the differentiator when everyone can hit baseline.

Core claims: memos get nodded at; felt research (video, silence in the all-hands) moves orgs. Prototypes are the new argument and the opportunity cost of high-fidelity ideas collapsed for managers. Player-coach / hands-on is table stakes for design leadership hiring; outside builds count. Building software got easier; growing an audience did not. Plan in words, build in phases, keep Figma as show-vs-tell stopgap, inject brand before the end. Designers still own problem framing, scrappy research, differentiation/delight; technical literacy helps steer agents; motion + brand engineering separate you when AI sameness is free.

Why it matters: concrete playbook for design leaders who still lead with research theater and now ship persuasion prototypes — plus an honest Matchmaker zero-to-one in Lovable and a hiring rubric for AI-era design orgs.
