## Takeaways (Granola)

Granola-weak: Notion episode Scratchpad while editing (no Granola Editing note). Normalized from that scratchpad — do not invent a Granola note:

### Design-led businesses / sweet spot of ignorance
- Design-led approach to healthcare
- Designers suited to pick big ambiguous / societal-level challenges
- Sweet spot of ignorance: know enough to add value, not so entrenched you can't see innovation
- Gaps in knowledge aren't barriers — learnable or outsourceable
- Every designer can start a company with discipline + appetite

### Bias to action
- Limiting factor is psychological shift not knowledge shift
- "don't wait for people to grace you with the opportunity. Just go and do it yourself"
- The second you do it, you are that thing

### Storytelling / inciting incident
- Even Titanic ideas fail if people around you don't see what you see
- People retain conflict + steps to overcome, not frameworks
- Arm others with the major conflict + way to overcome so they can rearticulate
