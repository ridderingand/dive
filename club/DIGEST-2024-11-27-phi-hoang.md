## Takeaways (Granola)

- Power of a brand that owns a single word (e.g. “curiosity”) as north star — if it evokes curiosity/wonder, medium can be retro illustration *or* a curious cat reading a book
- Perplexity brand = **creative consistency > visual consistency**; professional features can look serious while student surfaces look playful
- Listen to sales/support language with customers as the seed of ideas (e.g. needle in a haystack)
- Brand in “chapters” / seasons — currently imperfections + grainy textures; AI feels too polished so make it human; visual aesthetic will change significantly; oil paintings / retrofuturism now, future unknown (keeps talent excited)
- Keep brand open — internet has no single aesthetic; finance pros and high-school sports fans; Disney World of theme parks
- Crit with his 4-year-old — too much feedback seeks validation; kids tell it straight
- Process: deconstruct feature essence → AI brainstorm metaphors → FigJam storyboard for video → Midjourney for composition/type fuel; AI is great at metaphors
- Tools: Midjourney, Runway (image→video unlock), Luma (slot-machine), Krea backup; Henry Modisett / Smith & Diction context
