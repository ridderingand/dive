## Takeaways (Granola)

Granola-weak: Notion episode Scratchpad while editing (no Granola Editing note). Normalized from that scratchpad — do not invent a Granola note:

### Circular journeys (Audi logo)
- Journeys drawn as intersecting circles, not a linear waterslide
- Focus intersection points: features that move users between phases

### Ridiculously early hypothesis
- Design to find answers (not find answers then design)
- Strong narrative POV after ~1 week of kickoff; give clients something tangible to react to
- Co-evolve hypothesis vs big tada reveal

### Tarantino / unconstrained early concepting
- First 1–2 days ignore constraints; high-fidelity "art of the possible"
- Perceived barriers often mental self-censor; naming ("Tarantino", "ridiculously early hypothesis") makes the practice stick

### Storytelling
- Most important design skill; mid-week Loom / workshop framing matters as much as end-of-week pitch
- Boring presentations kill good ideas
- Leave room for wildcard exploration beyond empirical process

### Candidates
- Look for people who see the art of the possible and fight for highest outcome, not only pragmatic solutions
