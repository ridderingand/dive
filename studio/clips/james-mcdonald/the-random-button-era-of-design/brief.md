## Caption copy

**James:** Anytime I make these tools, I love to add a **random button** 'cause sometimes you can just get, like, weird stuff happening.
**Ridd:** Can you imagine, like, **18 months ago** if you saw this was part of your design process, the idea of **building a tool**? You're the person I associated with sweating the details of every little custom vector in **Figma**, and now you're hitting a random button in a custom tool that you **built yourself**.
**James:** Yeah. It's crazy. Even six months ago, honestly, I feel like I'm a **late bloomer** when it comes to this stuff. It disheartens me when I see designers not being all in on this stuff, because I know I felt that way, you know?
I've always handed design files off to engineers. From discovering ideas and inspiration, doing it this way just **empowers** a lot of that process.
There's this weird combination of things you've built. I'm not saying the random button is the be-all and end-all. But a bunch of this stuff, you could quite easily see in some sort of **brand guideline book**, a new design.
A lot of this stuff is where I definitely had a lot of **apprehension** when it came to exploring and playing around with tools like **Claude**.
This was the first project where I really had **fun** with that part of it, you know? Trying to take it from **zero to one**. It was just so much fun building this stuff, and that's where I got a taste for it. If I can build this, **I can build my own projects now**, you know?
