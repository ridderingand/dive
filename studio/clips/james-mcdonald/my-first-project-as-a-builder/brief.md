**Ridd:** Is this the **first project** that you've taken on as a freelancer where you're—

**James:** For the longest time, honestly, I was always so **jealous** of engineers being able to build ideas. Or not even jealous, sometimes **frustrated**, because maybe an engineer doesn't quite do what you envision, the idea you have in your mind.
