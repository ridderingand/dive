## Caption copy

**Ridd:** I quote **Soleio** too much on this show. He had this idea he talked about years ago, about how great designers **self-plagiarize**.

**James:** So I tried to use that original idea of that animation, and obviously it output something completely different, like here. I'm really happy I managed to **reuse that idea**. I don't know if I would have ever thought about that or done that in **Figma** first.

I think that's where **Claude** really helped. You can have an idea and it doesn't quite work, and you're like, "Right, I'll just **shelve that** for now." But then it came in handy here. I'm quite happy with how those folded back in.

**Ridd:** I even have my own story about that. I made multiple versions of the **Inflight** website, and one was only alive for a month. I had this dashed line that I massaged with Claude to have this **gradient pulse**. Claude called it the **Ethereal Connector**. It hasn't been live for so long, but so many times I've been like, "Hey, can you make that like the old Ethereal Connector?" And it just does something, and I'm like, "Oh, that's so sick."

**James:** And that old idea got **repurposed** in a way that fit perfectly for what I wanted here. That's the **magical part** of these tools, for me anyway. Being able to actually **animate** this stuff is just so exciting for me, you know?
